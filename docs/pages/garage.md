# My Garage

Create a build profile to organize information about the actual vehicle rather than relying only on a datalog.

## What to record

- Vehicle, year, chassis and engine details.
- Fuel and power-adder configuration.
- ECU and transmission/controller notes.
- Modifications, hardware limits, known issues and goals.
- Log history and what changed between sessions.

## Keeping your data

Garage and most saved app preferences are stored in the **current browser**. There is no cloud account or cross-device synchronization in this release.

Use the Garage export/backup functionality before changing phones or clearing your browser. Avoid saving private credentials in notes.

## Why it helps

A fuel pressure event on one setup may need a different investigation from the same apparent reading on another. The Garage profile helps you organize your real hardware and follow-up checks without the app inventing missing details.
