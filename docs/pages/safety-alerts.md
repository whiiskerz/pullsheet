# Warnings and confidence

SIGNAL is a read-only **screening and learning** tool. Its flags help direct your investigation; they do not certify mechanical condition or identify a repair with certainty.

## Examples of detected events

| Event | What it suggests reviewing |
| --- | --- |
| Lean / rich deviation | Actual versus target lambda, sensor validity, fuel cut and transient behavior |
| Relative pressure dip | Logged pressure in comparable running samples and sensor/regulator reference |
| Knock indication | Actual channel meaning, timing context, sensor state and configured protections |
| ECU warning | Reported status and any real ECU fault / protection configuration |

## What a priority score is not

It is **not** a standalone engine safety grade. The absence of an event may mean the relevant channel was never recorded or qualified for analysis.

## Before more high-load testing

If your log flags a possible high-load lean, pressure, knock, or protection issue, inspect the actual vehicle and ECU protections before relying on further loaded runs. Ask a qualified professional when needed.

**Never disable protections merely to silence an app alert.**
